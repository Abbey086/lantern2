Questions

Is Lantern therapy?

No. Lantern is a scripture reading aid — not counseling, diagnosis, or medical advice. In a crisis, contact 988 (US) or findahelpline.com.

Where does scripture come from?

Public-domain translations (WEB, KJV, ASV, BBE) fetched live from bible-api.com.

How does matching work?

Lantern reads what you share and matches it to scripture that fits. A built-in safety layer watches for crisis and harm and always surfaces care resources.

Where is my data?

On your device. Journal, settings and plan live in local storage. Export or erase anytime.

Hackathon build — plans are simulated locally, no card required.

Immediate support

I am not a mental health professional. If you are feeling overwhelmed or having thoughts of suicide, please reach out right now — people want to listen.

  • US: call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), 24/7
  • Text: HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line)
  • International: findahelpline.com
  • Immediate danger: call 911 or your local emergency number

Worried about hurting someone or breaking the law?

Lantern can't walk that road with you, but people can. Talking to a professional, clergy member, or trusted person today can change everything.

The verses Lantern shares are meant to offer hope and remind you of God's enduring love and the value of your life. Please also consider a trusted person or professional. Your life matters, and help is available.
